Previously, I have worked at [Advanced Micro Devices, Inc.](https://www.amd.com/en.html) as a Member of Technical Staff, [Qualcomm AI Research](https://www.qualcomm.com/research/artificial-intelligence/ai-research) as a Senior ML Engineer and [Amazon Lab126](https://amazon.jobs/en/teams/lab126/) Hardware Compute Group as a SysDE ML Compiler Engineer. I graduated with two research focussed masters from [University of Southern California](https://viterbischool.usc.edu/) in 2020 and an integrated dual degree from [Indian Institute of Technology, Kharagpur](http://www.ee.iitkgp.ac.in/) in 2017. At present, I am finishing up my doctorate from [University of Texas, Austin](https://www.ece.utexas.edu/) where my research focus is designing energy-efficient, robust, machine learning algorithms for generative AI edge applications.

## Skills

- Building, enhancing and deploying the ML compiler pipeline for autonomous vehicle hardware accelerators, enabling [full self-driving](https://news.gm.com/home.detail.html/Pages/topic/us/en/2025/feb/0225-supercruise.html?evar25=gm_com_autonomous_driving) at Cruise.
- Optimized ONNX Copilot models for AMD's AI Engine, owning ML graph optimizations and MLIR generation.
- Built the compiler stack for the Hexagon Tensor Processor that enabled seamless low-latency, high-throughput [generative AI inference on the edge](https://www.qualcomm.com/news/onq/2023/02/worlds-first-on-device-demonstration-of-stable-diffusion-on-android "Qualcomm AI Research deploys Stable Diffusion with 1B+ parameter foundation model on an edge device through full-stack AI optimization").
- Founding team member of the AZ1 Neural Edge Processor, optimizing the software stack to keep AI computations on the edge while maintaining throughput and reducing latency, e.g. [processing voice commands on device](https://techcrunch.com/2021/09/28/new-amazon-echo-devices-will-have-local-voice-processing-giving-users-more-privacy/ "New Amazon Echo devices have local voice processing").
- Model compression and quantization for LLMs and diffusion models on resource-constrained devices, down to microcontrollers. Published **EntroLLM** in **ICASSP 2026** and **EntroMCU** (invited) in **ISVLSI 2026**.
- Algorithms and architecture co-design for AI accelerators focussed towards training/inference with lesser compute/energy budget. Published a paper in **ICASSP 2020**.
- **Languages:** Python, C, C++, CUDA, MATLAB, Java, Shell, LaTeX, Assembly (AVR, ARM, MIPS) &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Frameworks:** PyTorch, TensorFlow, ONNX, StableHLO, MLIR
